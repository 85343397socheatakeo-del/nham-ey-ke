CATEGORY 1: QUICK & BUDGET (ម្ហូបរហ័ស & សន្សំសំចៃ - ក្រោម $2)
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Trei Chh'eur Chha Pongtea (ត្រីខកំប៉ុងឆាពងទា)
   - Cost: $1.25 (~5,000 Riels)
   - Cook Time: 10 mins
   - Student Hack: Canned sardine sauce makes its own rich gravy. No need for complicated seasoning!
   - Ingredients: 1 can of sardines/mackerel, 2 chicken/duck eggs, 1 shallot sliced, 1 bird chili, fresh lime, cilantro.
   - Steps:
     1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a small pan, saute sliced shallot and garlic until fragrant.
     2. Pour in canned fish with its tomato sauce; break fish into bite-sized chunks.
     3. Crack 2 eggs directly into pan; let set for 1 min then gently fold.
     4. Squeeze fresh lime juice, garnish with sliced chili and cilantro. Serve hot with steamed rice.

2. Pongtea Chien Chrok Trokoun (ពងទាចៀនជ្រកត្រកួន)
   - Cost: $0.80 (~3,200 Riels)
   - Cook Time: 8 mins
   - Student Hack: Water spinach (trokoun) gives crunchy sweetness and costs only 1000 riels a bunch at Phsar!
   - Ingredients: 3 eggs, 1 handful finely chopped water spinach, fish sauce, black pepper, cooking oil.
   - Steps:
     1. Whisk eggs with 1 tsp fish sauce, a pinch of sugar, and ground black pepper.
     2. Stir in chopped water spinach thoroughly.
     3. Heat oil on medium-high; pour egg mixture and fry until golden brown on both sides.
     4. Cut into triangles and pair with soy sauce and bird's eye chilies.

3. Chha Trasork Sach Chrouk (ឆាត្រសក់សាច់ជ្រូកចិញ្ច្រាំ)
   - Cost: $1.50 (~6,000 Riels)
   - Cook Time: 12 mins
   - Student Hack: Cucumbers remain juicy and don't need much oil. Great hydrating meal after long lecture classes.
   - Ingredients: 2 medium cucumbers sliced thinly, 100g minced pork (or tofu), 2 cloves garlic, oyster sauce.
   - Steps:
     1. Mince garlic and brown quickly in 1 tbsp hot oil.
     2. Add minced pork and stir-fry until cooked through.
     3. Toss in sliced cucumber and add 1 tbsp oyster sauce and a splash of water.
     4. Stir-fry on high heat for 2 minutes to keep cucumbers crisp.


CATEGORY 2: DORM & RICE COOKER (ម្ហូបឆ្នាំងដាំបាយ & ខ្ទះមួយ)
-----------------------------------------------------
4. Khor Sach Chrouk Pong Tea (ខសាច់ជ្រូកពងទា កម្រិតនិស្សិត)
   - Cost: $2.20 (~9,000 Riels)
   - Cook Time: 25 mins (Rice Cooker or Small Pot)
   - Student Hack: Press the "Cook" button on your rice cooker! Caramelize sugar right at the bottom of the pot.
   - Ingredients: 200g pork belly or shoulder, 2 hard-boiled eggs, 2 tbsp sugar, fish sauce, garlic, black pepper, coconut water or water.
   - Steps:
     1. In rice cooker, melt sugar with a few drops of water until it turns amber brown.
     2. Add garlic and pork pieces; stir to coat in rich caramel.
     3. Add 2 tbsp fish sauce, crushed black pepper, and pour coconut water to submerge meat.
     4. Add peeled boiled eggs, close rice cooker lid, and simmer until tender and glossy.

5. Babor Sor Nham Trei Ngiat (បបររសជាតិស្រាល ញ៉ាំជាមួយត្រីងៀត)
   - Cost: $1.40 (~5,500 Riels)
   - Cook Time: 20 mins
   - Student Hack: The ultimate exam-week recovery meal. Just put rice and 5x water in your rice cooker.
   - Ingredients: 1/2 cup Jasmine rice, 3 cups water or broth, fresh ginger strips, dried salt fish or fried egg, fried garlic.
   - Steps:
     1. Wash rice and place in rice cooker with generous water and ginger slivers.
     2. Turn on "Cook" or "Porridge" mode. Stir occasionally so rice grains don't stick to bottom.
     3. Pan-fry small pieces of dried fish or an omelette on your portable stove.
     4. Ladle silky congee into a bowl, sprinkle crispy garlic, and enjoy steaming hot.

6. Soup Sluk Bas Sach Chrouk (ស៊ុបស្លឹកបាសសាច់ជ្រូកចិញ្ច្រាំ)
   - Cost: $1.20 (~4,800 Riels)
   - Cook Time: 10 mins
   - Student Hack: Ivy gourd leaves (sluk bas) cook in under 60 seconds and are packed with vitamins.
   - Ingredients: 1 bowl washed ivy gourd leaves, 100g minced pork, 1 tsp salt, fish sauce, chicken bouillon, water.
   - Steps:
     1. Boil 2 cups of water in your pot or electric kettle/cooker.
     2. Season minced pork with pepper and drop spoonfuls into the boiling water.
     3. Skim off any surface foam, add fish sauce and bouillon powder to taste.
     4. Drop in fresh ivy gourd leaves, turn off heat immediately, and cover for 1 min.


CATEGORY 3: MOM'S COMFORT CLASSICS (រសជាតិស្នាដៃម៉ាក់)
-----------------------------------------------------
7. Chha Kh'nhei Sach Moan (ឆាខ្ញីសាច់មាន់ រសជាតិម៉ាក់)
   - Cost: $1.80 (~7,200 Riels)
   - Cook Time: 15 mins
   - Student Hack: Buy julienned fresh ginger from market aunties to save prep time. Fermented soy beans (soybean paste) give that signature mom flavor.
   - Ingredients: 200g chicken sliced, 1 cup shredded ginger, 1 tbsp fermented yellow soybean paste, garlic, palm sugar, fish sauce.
   - Steps:
     1. Saute smashed garlic and chicken until chicken changes color.
     2. Add fermented soybeans and palm sugar, stirring until deeply aromatic.
     3. Dump in all shredded ginger and stir-fry vigorously over high heat.
     4. Splash with fish sauce, cook for 3 more minutes until ginger is soft and fragrant.

8. Samlor Machu Kroeung Simple (សម្លម្ជូរគ្រឿង សាមញ្ញមិនបាច់បុកគ្រឿង)
   - Cost: $2.50 (~10,000 Riels)
   - Cook Time: 20 mins
   - Student Hack: Ask Phsar vendor for "Kroeung 1000 riel" (lemongrass paste). No heavy stone mortar needed in your rented room!
   - Ingredients: 150g beef or pork ribs, 2 tbsp yellow kroeung paste, morning glory stems, water celery, tamarind powder, fish paste (prohok).
   - Steps:
     1. Heat a small splash of oil, saute kroeung and dissolved prohok until aroma fills room.
     2. Add meat and sear until coated in fragrant golden paste.
     3. Pour water and bring to an energetic rolling boil; season with tamarind and sugar.
     4. Toss in morning glory and holy basil (sluk krasang / m'ras prov), remove from heat and serve with warm rice.

9. Chha Spey Kmao Sach Chrouk (ឆាស្ពៃខ្មៅខ្ទឹមស សាច់ជ្រូក)
   - Cost: $1.30 (~5,200 Riels)
   - Cook Time: 8 mins
   - Student Hack: High heat, don't overcook the greens. Retains sweet crunch and bright emerald color.
   - Ingredients: 1 bunch Bok Choy or Chinese cabbage, 50g sliced pork, 4 smashed garlic cloves, oyster sauce.
   - Steps:
     1. Separate vegetable stems from leaves.
     2. Fry smashed garlic in hot oil until golden; add pork slices.
     3. Add crisp stems first for 1 minute, then add leafy tops.
     4. Season with 1 tbsp oyster sauce and 1 pinch sugar. Toss 30 seconds and plate immediately.
